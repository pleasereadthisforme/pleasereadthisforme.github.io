# Brand usage

“Please read this for me”, the project's logos and its mascots identify this project. This document does not claim that any mark is registered.

You may accurately name the project, link to it, discuss or review it, and identify the origin of a fork. Preserve required copyright and license notices. Mascot images and motion code covered by [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) may be used, modified and redistributed under their license, including commercially.

Do not falsely describe an independent app, fork, website or product as an official release, or claim approval, sponsorship or affiliation that has not been granted. Clearly identify independently maintained or modified versions and their maintainer. If branding would mislead users about who operates or supports a product, distinguish it from the official project.

Software and asset licenses do not by themselves grant trademark rights. This guidance does not restrict lawful descriptive use, criticism, or rights granted by the applicable software or asset license. It is not a blanket prohibition on using or redistributing the mascot.
